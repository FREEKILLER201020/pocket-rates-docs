Reporting a problem

Include the app version, phone model, OS version, steps and expected result. Remove personal information from screenshots. TestFlight also provides its own feedback feature.

Rates and connectivity

The first launch requires a rate download. Subsequently the app can use its cache, with the rate date displayed. If a source does not support a currency, select another source or currency: the app does not silently substitute rates.

Calculator

Tap an amount. The = key calculates and closes the panel. Hiding the panel preserves the expression. AC clears it; percent divides the preceding number by 100.

Why a bank quotes a different amount

Rates are informational. Bank fees, spreads and transaction timing can change the actual amount. The app does not exchange money.

Appearance

Light, dark and device-color modes are available. Android uses the system palette where supported. On iPhone, system semantic colors and light/dark appearance are used; wallpaper colors and icon tint are not read. The current beta interface is Russian.
