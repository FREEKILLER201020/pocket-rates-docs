Privacy policy

This policy covers Pocket Rates for Android and iOS. Developer and responsible person: Kirill Burchenko. For privacy questions and deletion of support correspondence: kirill.burchenko@pm.me.

In brief

The app has no registration, advertising, built-in analytics or Pocket Rates backend. Calculations run on your device. Entered amounts, expressions and favorite currency lists are not sent to the developer or rate providers. To update rates, the app contacts the selected provider directly.

Data on your device

The app stores currencies and their order, the active currency, entered expression, theme and source settings, the background-refresh preference and cached rates locally. Clipboard contents are read only after an explicit paste action and written when copying; the app does not monitor the clipboard in the background.

Local data remains until cleared or the app is fully deleted. Offloading an iOS app may retain its data. Android/iOS system backups may include app data, and restoring a backup may restore it. Backup controls are provided by your operating system.

Network requests and recipients

When updating rates, the app sends the selected source an HTTPS request for a shared rate table. The provider and its infrastructure receive the outgoing IP address of the connection, request time, request address and parameters, and standard connection information and HTTP headers. A VPN or shared network gateway may supply the visible outgoing address.

Requests do not include amounts, expressions, favorites, your email address, advertising ID, contacts or GPS coordinates. The app does not determine your location. This does not assert what a provider could infer from an IP address.

Network information is necessary to deliver the request and response. Some providers also describe service protection and request limits. For example, ExchangeRate-API documents IP-based rate limiting. Retention and subsequent use of technical information by a recipient depend on its published rules; we do not claim that every source keeps no logs or uses the same retention period.

Documents and the role of each of the four providers are listed under Sources and their documentation. The NBRB link identifies API documentation, not an unverified privacy policy. Absence of a separate document does not mean absence of data processing.

When requests occur and your controls

Rates may update when launching or returning to the app, changing the source or requesting a manual refresh. Background refresh is enabled by default and can be disabled in Settings. It contacts the same selected source with the same information, not your entered amounts. The target interval is about six hours, but the operating system controls actual execution.

Disabling background refresh does not disable requests made while using the app. To use the app without requests, the network must be unavailable or app network access must be restricted using device controls where the operating system supports this. After the first successful download, calculations work with cached rates and their dates remain visible. A failed request does not automatically switch the provider.

External pages

Links open in an external browser only after you tap them. The destination's own rules also apply. These document pages are hosted on GitHub Pages: opening them sends GitHub a web request, and technical information is handled under its privacy statement. We have not added analytics, advertising scripts or data-collection forms to these pages.

Support and testing

If you contact support, we receive the sender address, message and attachments you choose to send. They are used to respond and investigate, not for advertising. Correspondence is retained while needed to handle the request and related follow-up matters; you can request deletion at the contact above. Mandatory legal retention can limit deletion. Email services also handle delivery and storage of messages.

Please avoid sending unnecessary personal or financial information. TestFlight/Google Play feedback and diagnostics are handled through the respective platform, and platform-provided reports may be available to the developer. This is separate from built-in Pocket Rates analytics.

Rights, security and changes

Rate requests use HTTPS. Contact the developer about the app's data handling; for information independently retained by a provider, its documents supply relevant contact routes. Rights of access, correction, deletion or objection depend on applicable law. This policy does not waive the developer's obligations or your statutory rights.

When adding a source or changing data practices, we update documents and applicable store disclosures, and provide notice or request consent where required. The revision date appears below. This policy does not replace providers' data-use terms. The current beta app interface is Russian.
