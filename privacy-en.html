Privacy policy

This policy covers Pocket Rates for Android and iOS. Operator: Kirill Burchenko. Privacy contact: kirill.burchenko@pm.me.

Local data

Your currencies, order, settings, entered expression and cached exchange rates are stored on your device. The developer does not receive your amounts, expressions or currency lists. There are no accounts, ads or built-in analytics. OS backups may include app data. Local data remains until removed or the app is uninstalled; restoring a backup may restore it.

Rate requests

HTTPS requests go directly to the selected provider: Bank of Russia, ExchangeRate-API, Frankfurter or National Bank of Belarus. Amounts and expressions are not included. Providers and their infrastructure receive network metadata, such as IP address and request time, subject to their own policies. Provider links are available on the Russian policy page and within the app.

Controls, support and beta testing

Background refresh can be disabled. Saved rates work offline after the first download. You can remove app-local data by uninstalling; OS backups have separate controls. External sites open only after a tap. If you contact support, we receive your email/message/attachments to respond and investigate; ask the contact above to delete correspondence. TestFlight/Google Play beta feedback and diagnostics are processed under those platforms' rules.

Changes

We update this page and the app when data practices change. The revision date is below. The current beta app interface is Russian.
