Sources and their documentation

All Pocket Rates calculations run on the device. The app downloads a shared table from the selected source without transmitting entered amounts. Providers are not switched automatically and may offer different currencies and rate dates.

Reading these documents

API documentation, usage terms and privacy provisions are identified separately. An API documentation link does not itself establish a separate privacy policy. Provider statements describe their practices; Pocket Rates does not guarantee absence of logging throughout third-party infrastructure.

The app is not affiliated with and does not represent these organizations. Their names identify data sources. Rates are informational; the app does not perform financial transactions. Read our policy for the information transmitted.
